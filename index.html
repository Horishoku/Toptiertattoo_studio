About

I’m a tattoo artist based in Halifax, Nova Scotia, Canada, focusing on fine line and black & grey tattoos.

I aim to create designs that fit each client’s body and story. My work balances clean linework, subtle shading, and a calm, focused approach to every session.

I currently work in a private studio in Halifax. Sessions are by appointment only.

FAQ

Do you require a deposit?

Yes. A non-refundable deposit is required to secure your appointment. The deposit goes toward the final price of your tattoo.

What is your minimum charge?

The minimum charge depends on the studio policy and design details. Please contact me with your idea for an estimate.

How should I prepare for my appointment?

Please sleep well, eat before your session, stay hydrated, and avoid alcohol or blood-thinning substances before your appointment.
